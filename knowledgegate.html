TALLENTEX is the acronym for ALLEN's Talent Encouragement Exam. TALLENTEX is a specially designed initiative to encourage young talent with cash prizes, scholarships, and mentoring by ALLEN.

Students studying in classes V, VI, VII, VIII, IX, & X in 2025-26 can appear in TALLENTEX 2026.

The TALLENTEX exam will be conducted in both offline and online formats.
The exam dates are 5th and 12th October, 2025 for offline (please refer to the zone-wise dates to know your exact exam date).
The online exam will be held on 5th, 7th, 11th and 12th October, 2025.

Students can apply for TALLENTEX 2026 through offline modes

A. Fill ONLINE FORM at https://www.tallentex.com/ and pay ONLINE.
B. Offline forms will be available at your school, if it has agreed for TALLENTEX 2026 registration. You can fill the form and deposit the fees.
C. Buy the Form: Visit any Allen Center, Info Center, or Associated School to purchase the form.
D. Fill and Submit: Complete the form and submit it to the location where you bought it.

To register for the online exam, students can visit the https://www.tallentex.com/, choose the online study mode, fill the form and click on submit to complete your registration.

The registration fee will be Rs. 300/- (Non-refundable). Upon successful payment, the student will be allotted a TALLENTEX Roll Number, a confirmation message on the registered mobile number, and be issued a fee acknowledgement receipt.

For the online exam, students can register using their mobile number and OTP. Once registration and payment are successfully completed, they will receive a confirmation message.
Please note: Roll numbers are not allotted for the online exam
